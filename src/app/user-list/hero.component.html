// ABOUT

I’m a Rwandan designer building digital products that feel clear, human, and built to last.

I work at the intersection of interface design, front-end engineering, and product thinking - turning ideas into polished web experiences with clarity, usability, impact, and craft.

02 / MY STORY

MY STORY

I started my journey in technology through Business Information Technology, where I learned how digital systems solve real-world problems. Over time, I became interested in frontend development, UI design, and creating smooth user experiences.

Today, I focus on building websites and applications that are simple, useful, and visually memorable. My goal is to create digital products that feel professional, easy to use, and meaningful.

03 / WHAT I WORK WITH

WHAT I WORK WITH

Design systems, interfaces, motion, and front-end builds.

  • Brand & visual direction Identity systems, layout rhythm, typography, color, and the little details that make a piece feel memorable.
  • Digital product design User flows, responsive screens, UI components, interaction states, and interfaces that are easy to scan and use.
  • Front-end development Angular, TypeScript, HTML, CSS, GSAP, responsive implementation, and animation that supports the experience.

04 / EXPERIENCE

EXPERIENCE

Hands-on project work across design and development.

  • Portfolio & personal systems Building a personal web presence with custom motion, structured sections, polished responsive behavior, and a strong visual identity.
  • Internship practice Turning learning into working interfaces: reading requirements, shaping components, improving UI clarity, and shipping usable front-end features.
  • Creative experiments Exploring animation, glass effects, editorial layouts, and interactive details that give projects personality without hurting usability.

05 / HOW I WORK

HOW I WORK

Question first, build carefully, refine until it feels right.

  • Understand the why I start by clarifying the goal, the audience, and what the work needs to make someone feel or do.
  • Shape the system I organize the layout, components, states, and visual rules so the experience can stay consistent as it grows.
  • Polish the details I tune spacing, motion, responsiveness, and copy until the result feels clean, intentional, and complete.
